Here is what you should know about me

I have always been the person who notices the details, asks too many questions and wants to understand why people love the things they love.

Marketing gave me somewhere to put all of that curiosity. I get to study people, brands and culture, then use what I learn to create something meaningful.

Sometimes that becomes a video. Sometimes it is an article, a campaign idea, a podcast conversation or an entire brand world. I like having more than one way to tell a story.

Most of my ideas live around beauty, fashion and lifestyle, but what really interests me is the person on the other side. What catches their attention? What feels honest to them? What will they remember later?

01Location and availabilityWhere are you based?+

I am based in Egypt and available for remote projects with clients and brands worldwide.

02Collaboration typeDo you accept gifted collaborations?+

I consider gifted collaborations selectively. It depends on the product, the brand and whether the opportunity feels relevant to my content and audience.

03Content usageCan brands use your content for advertisements?+

Paid advertising and other usage rights require a separate agreement. We can discuss the platforms, duration and intended use before the project begins.

04FeedbackHow do revisions work?+

The number of revisions depends on the project and will be agreed before work begins. This keeps the feedback process clear for both of us.

05TimingWhat is your turnaround time?+

Turnaround time depends on the type and size of the project. Once I understand what you need, I can provide a clear timeline before we begin.

06Industry fitDo you work outside beauty and fashion?+

Yes, I am open to working with other industries. It depends on the nature of the project, the brand and whether my skills and approach are a good fit.
